0.4.5
=====

Fixed
-----

- ``KMeans`` crashed with ``ValueError: Probabilities do not sum to 1`` from NumPy when the data
  had fewer distinct points than ``n_clusters``, for example identical points. Once every distinct
  point was a centroid, the k-means++ weights were all zero. The remaining initial centroids are
  now drawn uniformly, some of them coincide, and a ``RuntimeWarning`` says how many distinct
  points there are. Results on other data are unchanged.
- ``LogisticRegression``, ``RidgeClassifier``, ``LassoClassifier`` and ``ElasticNetClassifier``
  silently trained on a meaningless loss when the labels were not ``0`` and ``1`` (for example
  ``{1, 2}``, ``{-1, 1}`` or three classes), because the cross-entropy assumed 0/1 targets. They
  now take the two distinct values in ``y`` as ``classes_``, treat the larger one as the positive
  class, and ``predict`` returns the original labels. ``predict_proba`` is the probability of
  ``classes_[1]``. A ``y`` with fewer or more than two classes raises ``ValueError``. Results for
  ``{0, 1}`` labels are unchanged.
