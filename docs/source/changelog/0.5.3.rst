0.5.3
=====

Performance
-----------

- ``LogisticRegression``, ``RidgeClassifier``, ``LassoClassifier`` and ``ElasticNetClassifier``
  no longer go through the dispatch layers of ``np.sum`` and ``np.clip`` in every iteration of
  the gradient descent, and the labels are converted to floats once. Fitting is about 20%
  faster for ``RidgeClassifier``, ``LassoClassifier`` and ``ElasticNetClassifier`` and about
  10% faster for ``LogisticRegression`` on a data set of 500 samples and 30 features. The
  fitted coefficients, the intercept and the number of iterations are identical bit for bit.
  (#74)

Development
-----------

- ``benchmarks/asv`` compares the speed of two versions of pyml with ``asv``, and the
  ``dev-bench`` dependency group installs it. It times ``Ridge``, ``DecisionTreeRegressor``
  and the four logistic models. (#68, #74)
- ``pyproject.toml`` and ``docs/source/conf.py`` explain their settings in comments, and the
  README links the guides for contributors. (#70)
- The unused ``ignore_rules`` option of ``rstcheck`` is removed. The checks do not change. (#72)
- The examples use ``pyml-datasets`` 0.23.0 (it was 0.10.0). (#76)
